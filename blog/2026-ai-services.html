不卖软件卖服务：用 AI 接单赚钱的 4 种活法Don't Sell Software, Sell Services: 4 Ways to Earn with AI

2026.10.21 — 4 种模式、真实报价、每单多久，以及第一单怎么来2026.10.21 — 4 models, real prices, time per job, and how the first client finds you

为什么很多人做不出产品，却能靠 AI 赚到钱Why Lots of People Who Can't Build Products Still Earn with AI

"做一个产品，然后靠订阅赚钱"是媒体最爱讲的路径，但它对个人其实极不友好：产品要开发、要推广、要维护，周期 3–6 个月，而且大部分会死在第 60 天。而卖服务这条路短得多——客户带着明确需求来找你，你用 AI 把交付时间压到原来的 1/3，收钱，走人。 "Build a product, earn from subscriptions" is the path the media loves, and it's brutal for individuals: you must develop, promote, and maintain, over three to six months, and most die around day 60. Selling a service is far shorter — the client arrives with a specific request, AI cuts your delivery time to a third, you get paid and move on.

我自己两种都做过。2024 年我靠 11 单服务赚到 ¥23,000（平均每单 ¥2,100，用时 6–9 小时）；同期做的第一个订阅产品，做了 4 个月，月收入才 ¥900，还得天天维护。2025 年我重新算了一笔账：同样的 100 小时，服务模式赚 ¥12,000，订阅模式赚 ¥1,200。 I've done both. In 2024 I earned ¥23,000 from 11 service jobs (about ¥2,100 each, 6–9 hours apiece); my first subscription product from the same period took four months to reach ¥900 a month — and needed daily maintenance. In 2025 I ran the comparison again: for the same 100 hours, services paid ¥12,000 and the subscription paid ¥1,200.

但服务模式有自己的坑：它不"睡后收入"，你停手就没钱；而且客户要的是结果，不是"我用 AI 做的"。这篇文章讲的就是怎么绕开这两个坑——把服务做成可复制的流程，再决定要不要把高频的那部分产品化。 Services have their own traps: no income while you sleep, and clients pay for results, not for the fact that AI was involved. This article is about dodging both: turn the work into a repeatable process, then decide whether the most frequent part deserves to become a product.

先卖服务，再考虑做产品。服务让你知道客户到底要什么——这比任何用户调研都准，因为它已经付了钱。 Sell the service first; consider the product later. Services tell you what clients actually want — more accurately than any survey, because they've already paid.

模式 1：内容代写与改写——最快拿到第一单Model 1: Content and Rewriting — The Fastest First Pay

卖什么What you sell

不是"我帮你写文章"，而是"我把你的 3000 字口语稿改成 5 条小红书图文，每条 80 字带钩子"。把交付物写成一句能被验收的话，客户才知道买的是什么。Not "I'll write your article" but "I'll turn your 3,000-word voice-note transcript into 5 Xiaohongshu posts, 80 characters each with a hook". State the deliverable as one acceptance-checkable sentence so the client knows what they're buying.

怎么做得快How to do it fast

流程固定成四步，熟客一单 40 分钟：① 收集原始素材（客户的旧内容、录音、产品链接）② 让 AI 输出 3 个不同角度的版本 ③ 你做"人味"改写（这是唯一不能省的一步，见后面的章节）④ 按客户的语气模板交付。我的实际用时分布是 10 / 5 / 20 / 5 分钟。Four fixed steps; a repeat client takes 40 minutes: 1) collect raw material (old posts, recordings, product links); 2) ask the model for three different angles; 3) you do the human pass — the one step you cannot skip; 4) deliver in the client's template. My actual split is 10 / 5 / 20 / 5 minutes.

怎么报价How to price

按"篇"不按"小时"。新手报价 ¥120/篇（800 字图文）或 ¥250/篇（1500 字长文），连续做满 3 单并且客户没还价之后，可以提到 ¥200 / ¥380。我第一年接了 11 单，只有一单还过价，说明这个价位对中小客户是能接受的。Price per piece, not per hour. Beginners: ¥120 per 800-character post, or ¥250 per 1,500-character article. After three jobs with no pushback, raise to ¥200 / ¥380. I did 11 jobs in my first year and only one client negotiated, which suggests the price is acceptable to small businesses.

把"改写"当入口Use rewriting as the door in

新客户不会一上来就给你 ¥2,000 的自动化项目。先接一单 ¥120 的改写，交付好，然后问他："你每周都要发这些内容吗？要不要我做个自动化的工具，以后你只管审核？"——我 11 单里有 4 单是从 ¥120 的改写单升级成 ¥2,000+ 的自动化搭建的。这叫从服务里长出产品，比你凭空猜需求靠谱得多。New clients won't hand you a ¥2,000 automation project on the first message. Take a ¥120 rewrite, deliver well, then ask: "Do you post this every week? Want me to build something so you only approve it?" Of my 11 jobs, 4 grew from a ¥120 rewrite into a ¥2,000+ build. That's how products grow out of services — far more reliable than guessing requirements.

模式 2：资料整理与表格自动化——毛利最高的体力活Model 2: Data Cleanup & Spreadsheet Automation — The Best-Paying Manual Work

这个模式解决的是一件非常具体的事：客户每周要花几个小时手动整理一张乱表。销售把订单抄进表、运营把问卷导出来、财务把两个系统对账——这些活重复、规则明确、最适合让 AI 干。 This model solves one very specific problem: the client spends several hours every week hand-fixing a messy sheet. Sales re-typing orders into a sheet, ops exporting survey results, finance reconciling two systems — repetitive, rule-based, and perfect AI work.

我的做法是先做一次"整理服务"，再谈自动化：第一次上门，把客户现在的表结构原样跑一遍，产出整理好的版本和一份操作说明，收费 ¥300–¥500。这单的价值不在钱，在于你摸清了他的数据长什么样。然后第二步再报价"我把这个过程做成一个按钮，你每周点一下"——自动化报价 ¥1,200–¥3,000。 My approach: do one cleanup job first, then talk about automation. On the first visit, run their current sheet structure once, deliver a cleaned version plus a written procedure, and charge ¥300–¥500. The value isn't the money — it's that you now know what their data looks like. Then quote the automation: "I can turn that process into one button you click weekly" — ¥1,200–¥3,000.

注意这个顺序：没有第一步的整理，你根本不知道第二步该自动化什么，做出来的东西客户不会用。我踩过一次坑——接了一个"自动生成报价单"的单子，没看原始数据就动手，结果客户的 Excel 模板有 7 个 sheet、合并单元格、有密码保护，做出来的东西他根本打不开。最后赔了 3 天时间只收 ¥800。 Note the order: without the cleanup step you don't know what to automate, and the client won't use what you build. I made this mistake once — took a "generate quotes automatically" job, started building before looking at the source data, and found the client's workbook had seven sheets, merged cells, and password protection. The output wouldn't even open for him. I lost three days and collected only ¥800.

模式 3：求职材料打磨——需求最痛、复购最强Model 3: Job Application Materials — Sharpest Pain, Strongest Repeat

这是我做得最顺的模式，因为客户当天就要结果，愿意付溢价。我的套餐是：简历重写 ¥199（从 90 分钟到 2.5 小时）、简历 + 求职信 + 自我介绍 ¥399、一对一模拟面试 45 分钟 ¥299。 This is my smoothest model, because clients need the result today and will pay a premium. My menu: résumé rewrite ¥199 (from 90 minutes to 2.5 hours), résumé + cover letter + intro ¥399, and a 45-minute mock interview ¥299.

但有个必须提前想清楚的问题：你在替人包装经历，这是有职业风险的。我的边界写得很清楚——我只做表达优化（把"负责了社群运营"改成"运营 200 人社群，30 天内涨粉 2,400，转化率 8%"），不编造经历、不改时间、不写你没做过的事。这条线越过了，客户被 HR 追问时会找你麻烦，而且这类风险直接砸掉口碑。 One risk to settle up front: you're polishing someone's claims, which carries professional liability. My boundary is explicit — I optimise expression ("ran community operations" becomes "ran a 200-member community, grew it 2,400 in 30 days at 8% conversion"), and I never fabricate experience, alter dates, or invent things you didn't do. Cross that line and you get in trouble the moment an interviewer probes, and that kills your reputation outright.

顺带一提：这个需求会催生真正的产品Aside: this demand will spawn a real product

2024 年我靠这个模式接到 11 单之后，做了自己的「AI 简历优化器」，月收入 ¥7,260。顺序不能反——先有服务才知道该做什么产品，直接凭想象做产品的人，最后都在做没人要的功能。服务是产品最便宜的市场调研。After 11 jobs in this model in 2024, I built my "AI Resume Optimizer", which now earns ¥7,260 a month. The order matters — services tell you what to build; people who skip straight to products end up building features nobody wanted. Services are the cheapest market research there is.

模式 4：小型业务自动化搭建——单价最高，也最容易翻车Model 4: Small-Business Automation — Highest Price, Easiest Way to Burn Out

这是"AI 编程"最直接的变现方式：给一家小公司做一个能自动跑的系统。常见需求是"每天把客户表单自动汇总成一张表并发到群里"、"客服微信来了自动分类"、"合同到期前 30 天提醒"。 This is the most direct monetisation of "AI coding": build a system that runs itself for a small company. Typical asks: "summarise the customer form into one sheet every morning and post it to the group chat", "auto-tag incoming WeChat messages", "remind me 30 days before a contract expires".

报价按价值不按工时：客户每月因此省下 6 小时人工，¥6,000 的报价（相当于每月省 ¥120 的成本）是能谈下来的。我的一单真实案例是给一家 12 人的小公司做"报价单 + 合同 + 客户跟进"的自动生成，报价 ¥6,800，用了 11 天，其中真正的编码时间约 6 小时，其余是沟通、试错和改需求。 Price by value, not hours: if the client saves 6 hours a month, ¥6,000 works out to ¥120/hour of their saved labour, and that closes. One real example: an automation for a 12-person firm generating quotes, contracts, and follow-ups, priced at ¥6,800 and delivered in 11 days — about 6 hours of actual coding, the rest being calls, debugging, and scope changes.

翻车点几乎从来不是技术，是需求没写下来。这一单我改了 3 轮范围：第 1 轮客户说"要能生成合同"，第 2 轮加了"按客户的行业自动换条款"，第 3 轮又加了"要能导出 PDF"。如果你不在开工前把这些写成一页纸的清单，这 3 轮会变成 6 轮，而且每一轮你都在免费改。 Things almost never go wrong technically — they go wrong because the requirement was never written down. This job went through three scope rounds: round 1 "it should generate contracts", round 2 "swap clauses by industry", round 3 "also export PDF". If you don't capture those as a one-page checklist before starting, three rounds become six — and every round is a free change you made.

接单本身：报价、需求单与验收Doing the Job: Quotes, Specs, and Acceptance

服务模式里最容易亏钱的地方不是技术，是边界没写清楚。客户以为"改一下"是改 1 句，你以为是 1 句，结果他改了 7 版。我的做法是每单开工前发一份一页纸的需求单，双方确认后再动手。 The way service jobs lose money isn't technical — it's undefined scope. The client thinks "a small tweak" is one line; you think one line; they come back seven times. My rule: every job starts with a one-page spec that both sides confirm before any work begins. 

# 需求单（每次接单都发一份）
## 交付物
- 5 条小红书图文，每条 80 字以内，含 1 个钩子和 2 个话题标签

## 修改次数
- 含 2 轮修改（每轮 24 小时内反馈）。超出按 ¥30/条计

## 客户提供
- 原始口述稿 1 份、产品链接 3 个、参考账号 2 个

## 明确不做
- 不做封面图、不做评论区维护、不做账号运营

## 验收
- 按客户语气模板交付；发布 2 条后无重大修改即视为通过
- 交付后 7 天内因事实性错误免费修改 1 次

## 费用与时间
- ¥600（5 条），预计 2 天内交初稿，2 天后交终稿

这张单子里有四个东西缺一不可：交付物（什么算做完）、修改次数（防止无限返工）、不做什么（防止范围蔓延）、验收方式（谁说了算）。最后一段"费用与时间"里那句"2 天内交初稿，2 天后交终稿"也很重要——把交付节奏写进单子，你就不会被客户在第三天催到崩掉。 Four things in that spec are non-negotiable: deliverable (what "done" means), revision rounds (stops infinite rework), out of scope (stops scope creep), and acceptance (who decides). The last line matters too — "draft in 2 days, final 2 days after" puts the pace in writing, so you aren't chased on day three.

一个定价公式A pricing formula

报价 =（客户原本要花的时间 × 0.4）+ ¥200 底价。
意思是：AI 帮你省掉的时间，你只收其中 40%，剩下的给客户当"用了 AI 还不贵"的说服力。客户永远觉得划算，你也不会因为降价而饿死。试过一次 ¥2,000 的单，客户还主动说"比我想的便宜，可以再加一个功能吗"。Price = (the time it would have taken them × 0.4) + a ¥200 floor.
In other words: of the time AI saves you, you charge 40% and give the client the rest as evidence that "AI isn't even expensive". The client always feels they got a bargain and you don't starve. On a ¥2,000 job the client said "that's cheaper than I expected — can we add one more thing?"

从服务到产品：什么时候该把活儿变成软件From Service to Product: When to Turn the Work Into Software

服务做到一定程度会变成"高级外包"——你卖的是时间，天花板很低。我的判断标准有三个，同时满足两个就该产品化： Services eventually become "premium outsourcing" — you're selling hours, and the ceiling is low. My rule needs three signals, and two of them means it's time to productise:

  • 同一个需求被问了 3 次以上——说明它是个真需求，不是个性化定制。The same request has come up 3+ times — it's a real need, not a customisation.
  • 每次交付有 70% 以上的工作是重复的——产品化能把这部分变成一次投入。70%+ of each job is the same steps — productising converts repetition into a one-off investment.
  • 客户愿意预付或包月——说明他买的是持续结果，不是一次性的活儿。Clients are willing to prepay or go monthly — they're buying an ongoing outcome, not a one-off task.

我的真实路径就是这样：2024 年 11 单服务里，有 6 单都是"改简历"，而且 4 个人问过"有没有更方便的方式"。我花了 3 周做了那个简历优化器，现在它每月 ¥7,260。产品不是我"想"出来的，是客户用钱包投票投出来的。 My own path: of 11 jobs in 2024, six were résumé rewrites, and four people asked "is there an easier way?" I spent three weeks building the resume optimizer; it now earns ¥7,260 a month. I didn't invent the product — customers elected it with their wallets.

结论Conclusion

对普通人来说，AI 接单是目前确定性最高、启动成本最低的一条路：你不需要有产品，不需要有流量，只需要能解决一个具体问题并在一周内交付。第一单的目标不是赚钱，是验证你能不能稳定交付。我认识的人里，靠这条路在三个月内做到月入 ¥5,000 的有 6 个，做到月入 ¥2 万的只有 1 个——那个人的做法很简单：只接一个行业的活，把流程写成 SOP，然后招人一起做。For ordinary people, AI services are the highest-certainty, lowest-startup-cost path available: you don't need a product or an audience, only the ability to solve one concrete problem and deliver inside a week. The first job isn't about money — it's about proving you can deliver reliably. Of the people I know, six reached ¥5,000 a month this way within three months, and only one reached ¥20,000. That person's method was simple: serve one industry only, write the process as an SOP, then bring in help.
