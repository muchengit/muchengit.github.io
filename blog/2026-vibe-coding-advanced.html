Vibe Coding 进阶：当 AI 写的代码超过 10 万行之后Advanced Vibe Coding: When AI-Written Code Passes 100,000 Lines

2026.08.28 — 从"能跑"到"能维护"，一份踩过坑才写得出的实战指南2026.08.28 — From "it runs" to "it can be maintained": a field guide written after the mistakes

10 万行，是一个什么概念What 100,000 Lines Actually Means

2026 年 3 月，我接手了一个"发票管理 SaaS"的项目。到 6 月它长成了这样：前端 4.1 万行、后端 5.8 万行、脚本 2.2 万行、测试 1.4 万行，合计约 13.5 万行。其中 91% 是 AI 写的——准确地说，是我在旁边盯着 AI 写的。 In March 2026 I took over an "invoice management SaaS" project. By June it looked like this: 41k lines of frontend, 58k of backend, 22k of scripts, 14k of tests — about 135,000 lines in total. Ninety-one percent of it was AI-written; more precisely, AI-written while I watched.

就在这三个月里，我遇到了"vibe coding"教程里从来不提的那一面：代码能跑，但项目开始失控了。不是 AI 写不出正确的代码，恰恰相反，它写得又快又漂亮。问题是它对整个项目的理解开始出现缝隙——同一个约定在三个地方被理解成三种意思，同一个函数被实现了两遍，改完调用方忘了改类型定义。 During those three months I met the side of vibe coding that tutorials never mention: the code runs, but the project starts to slip out of your hands. It isn't that the AI can't write correct code — quite the opposite, it writes it fast and beautifully. The problem is that its grasp of the project develops gaps: the same convention understood three different ways in three places, the same function implemented twice, a caller updated while the type definition was forgotten.

先说最重要的一句：10 万行不是问题，"一次让 AI 看的东西太多"才是问题。行数只是症状。你真正需要管理的是上下文——你每个会话喂给模型的信息量。The single most important thing to say up front: 100,000 lines isn't the problem — "too much for the AI to look at once" is.Line count is just a symptom. What you actually need to manage is context: how much information you feed the model each session.

先做个粗算。13.5 万行代码，按平均每行 8 个 token 算，全部塞进上下文大约需要 108 万 token。2026 年的模型窗口虽然已经普遍到 20 万到 100 万 token，但即使窗口够大，也不代表塞满之后还能正常工作——系统提示、工具定义、历史对话、文件读取记录都要占位置。更关键的是，就算技术上装得下，超过一定规模之后，模型对上下文的"注意力"会明显衰减。 Let's do rough arithmetic. 135,000 lines at roughly 8 tokens per line is about 1.08 million tokens to hold the whole project. Model windows in 2026 commonly run from 200k to 1M tokens, but a window being big enough doesn't mean the model still works well when it's full — the system prompt, tool definitions, conversation history, and file-read logs all take up room. And even when it technically fits, past a certain size the model's attention to the context degrades noticeably.

把上下文窗口当成内存，不是硬盘。内存不够会崩，硬盘不够只是慢——而 AI 的失败方式更像内存不够：不是变慢，是直接开始胡说。 Think of the context window as RAM, not disk. Running out of RAM crashes; running out of disk just gets slow. And the way AI fails resembles running out of RAM: it doesn't get slower — it starts making things up.

第一步：让 AI 只看它该看的部分Step 1: Make the AI See Only What It Should

现在的主流 AI 编程工具都提供了限定范围的能力，但很多人只用其中一种，用错了地方。三种方式各有明确的适用场景，我按我实际的使用频率从高到低排。 Every major AI coding tool now lets you scope what the model sees, but most people use only one of them, in the wrong place. Here's how I actually use the three, ordered by frequency.

1）@folder：我的主力，占日常任务的 90%1) @folder: My default, ~90% of daily tasks

指定一个目录，只让它读这个目录。这是最精确的上下文控制——你把"这次任务的全部世界"压缩到 2000 到 4000 行，AI 的表现会立刻变好。关键前提是你的目录必须按职责切干净，否则你只是把混乱缩小了。 Point it at one directory and let it read only that. This is the tightest control you can get — you compress "the entire world of this task" into 2,000–4,000 lines and the AI immediately improves. The prerequisite is that your directories must be cleanly cut by responsibility; otherwise you've just shrunk the mess.

2）@codebase：只用来"找东西"，不用来"干活的"2) @codebase: For finding things, not for doing the work

全库语义检索。在 13 万行的项目上跑一次要 40 到 70 秒，而且会返回大量不相关的片段。它真正的用途是回答"这个功能到底在哪儿实现过"这类查找问题。用它来生成代码，等于把整个项目倒进上下文，正是我们要避免的。 Repo-wide semantic search. On a 135k-line project it takes 40–70 seconds and returns a lot of irrelevant chunks. Its real job is answering "where was this feature implemented before?" Use it to generate code and you're pouring the whole project into the context — exactly what we're trying to avoid.

3）分层目录：物理上的上下文隔离3) Layered directories: physical context isolation

这是最根本的一条。目录结构就是你的上下文策略——一个目录对应一个职责，AI 的一次会话就对应一个目录。这样你不需要每次都提醒它"别看别的"，结构本身已经替你说了。 This is the most fundamental one. Your directory layout is your context strategy — one directory, one responsibility, one AI session. Then you never have to say "don't look at anything else"; the structure says it for you.

按"一个人一次能读完"来切目录Cut Directories by "What One Person Reads in One Sitting"

判断标准不是"逻辑上属于同一类"，而是"我需要理解它才能改它吗"。需要一起改的放一个目录，不需要的分开。上面那个结构里，src/domain/ 约 5 千行，src/api/ 约 3 千行，都是单次会话能吃下的规模。The test isn't "do these belong to the same category" but "do I need to understand one to change the other." Things that change together go together; everything else splits. In the layout above, src/domain/ is about 5k lines and src/api/ about 3k — both comfortably within one session.

先给 AI 一个"我该看哪"的入口Give the AI a Single "Start Here" Entry Point

在规则文件里写清楚每类任务的入口目录，比如"改接口看 src/api/，改业务规则看 src/domain/"。这样连你自己都不用每次重新想"这次该让它读哪儿"。In your rules file, map task types to entry directories: "API changes start in src/api/; business-rule changes start in src/domain/." Then even you don't have to re-decide "where should it look this time."

会话结束就换新会话Start a New Session When the Task Ends

不要在一个会话里连做三件事。我的习惯是：一次会话 = 一个可交付的小任务。做完、开新会话、重新读规则文件。这个动作在 2026 年几乎是零成本，但它把我遇到"推倒重来"的次数从每周三四次降到一个月一次。Don't do three things in one session. My rule: one session equals one deliverable-sized task. Finish, open a new session, re-read the rules file. The action costs almost nothing in 2026, and it took my "rewrites from scratch" incidents from three or four a week to about one a month.

第二步：给 AI 写一份"项目说明文档"Step 2: Write a Project Brief for the AI

这就是大家说的 CLAUDE.md、AGENTS.md、.cursor/rules、.windsurfrules、.github/copilot-instructions.md——名字不同，作用一样：把"你脑子里那套规矩"变成 AI 每次都会读的文件。 This is what people mean by CLAUDE.md, AGENTS.md, .cursor/rules, .windsurfrules, or .github/copilot-instructions.md — different names, one job: turning the rules in your head into a file the AI reads every time.

大部分人写失败，是因为写成了说明书：把项目从头到尾讲一遍，两百行，AI 读完什么也记不住。好的规则文件只写"不知道就会做错"的东西。能被代码本身看出来的信息（函数名、目录名）不要写，能被 linter 和类型检查发现的错误也不要写。 Most people fail at this by writing a manual: a full tour of the project, 200 lines, of which the AI remembers nothing. A good rules file contains only what the AI would get wrong without it. Don't write what the code already shows (function names, directory names), and don't write what a linter or type checker already catches. 

# CLAUDE.md

## 1. 这是什么
发票管理 SaaS。2 名开发者，没有专职 QA。目标是稳定，不是优雅。

## 2. 技术栈与版本（不要升级）
- Node 20.11 / Next.js 14.2（App Router）

- 测试 Vitest 1.6，用 expect 风格，不要用 jest 语法

- src/domain/    纯业务规则，禁止 import 任何 UI 或数据库代码
- src/api/       只做参数校验和转发，业务逻辑一律不写在这里
- src/features/  页面代码，一个子目录对应一个业务功能
- src/infra/     第三方 API 封装，密钥只从 process.env 读

## 4. 不可违反的约定
- 金额一律用整数「分」存储，字段名以 Cents 结尾，禁止浮点

- 注释用中文，标识符用英文

## 5. 怎么跑
npm run dev        本地起服务
npm test           跑全部测试，必须全绿
npm run typecheck  类型检查，必须无错误

- src/infra/ocr.ts 的重试逻辑不要动，OCR 服务商限流 2 QPS
- src/api/export-legacy.ts 已废弃，新代码不要引用
为什么是 60 行这个上限Why 60 Lines Is the Ceiling

这是我自己调出来的数。我的规则文件一开始有 180 行，里面 70% 是重复的"要写测试""注释用中文"这类 AI 本来就会做的事。删到 60 行之后，出错率反而下降了——因为每条都足够"刺耳"，AI 不会忽略。规则文件要减，不要加。每加一条，就把已有的挤出去。I tuned this number myself. My rules file started at 180 lines, 70% of it redundant reminders like "write tests" and "comment in Chinese" that the AI already does. Trimming it to 60 lines lowered my error rate, because every remaining line was sharp enough to notice. Rules files should be subtracted, not added to.Every line you add pushes another one out.

还有一个容易忽略的细节：规则文件本身也要版本控制，并且每次踩坑之后立刻补一条。我那个项目里有 11 条约定，每一条后面都标注了来源，比如"（3 月 12 日：金额用分存储，否则对账差 0.01）"。这样三个月后的你一眼就知道这条为什么存在，也能判断它是否已经过时。 One easily missed detail: the rules file belongs in version control, and you add a line the moment you hit a bug. My project has 11 conventions, each annotated with its origin — for example "(Mar 12: store money in cents, otherwise reconciliation drifts by 0.01)." Three months later you can see instantly why a rule exists and whether it's still relevant.

第三步：让 AI 写测试Step 3: Have the AI Write the Tests

"让 AI 写测试"这句话本身没有信息量。同样是写测试，提示词里少一句话，结果就可能是"它写了一个空的 it() 就算完成"。我用的提示词范式有五个固定部分，缺一不可： "Have the AI write tests" carries almost no information. The same request with one line missing can produce an empty it() and a green checkmark. My prompt template has five fixed parts, and none of them is optional.

  1. 限定读取范围：明确写出"只读这两个文件"。这是防止它顺手大改的关键。Scope the reads: state "read only these two files." This is what stops it from making sweeping edits on the way.
  2. 先列场景再写代码：要求它先输出要覆盖的场景清单，你确认后再让它写。这一步花 30 秒，能省掉一小时返工。List the cases first: have it output the scenarios it will cover, you approve, then it writes. Thirty seconds here saves an hour of rework.
  3. 规定风格：测试框架、断言风格、路径写法，全部写死。Pin the style: test framework, assertion style, import paths — all specified.
  4. 要求它自己跑："写完运行 npx vitest run 路径，把失败的用例原样贴给我"，而不是"确保测试通过"。Make it run them: "then run npx vitest run path and paste the failures verbatim" — not "make sure the tests pass."
  5. 发现问题只报告、不修改：这一条最容易被忽略，但它能保护你的代码不被 AI 顺手改掉。Report problems, don't fix them: the most-missed clause, and the one that keeps the AI from quietly rewriting your code.
先写测试，不要写实现。

目标：给 src/domain/invoice.ts 的 calculateTax() 补测试。

要求：
1. 只读 src/domain/invoice.ts 和 src/domain/types.ts，不要读其他文件
2. 先列出你要覆盖的场景清单给我确认，我确认后再写代码
3. 用 Vitest 的 describe / it / expect 风格，import 用相对路径
4. 每个用例写一行中文说明
5. 写完后运行 npx vitest run src/domain/invoice.test.ts，
   把失败的用例原样贴给我
6. 如果发现实现本身有 bug，只报告，不要修改实现

这套范式在我的项目里跑了一个月，测试覆盖率从 11% 涨到 43%，而且新增功能的测试基本都是一次写对的。以前我让 AI 直接写实现，再回头补测试——覆盖率上去了，但我自己也不知道哪些用例是真的在测东西。现在是先写测试，测试本身就是需求的另一种表述。 Running this template for a month took test coverage from 11% to 43%, and tests for new features mostly came out right on the first pass. I used to have the AI write the implementation and backfill tests afterward — coverage went up, but I had no idea which cases were actually testing anything. Writing tests first means the test is a second form of the requirement.

第四步：让 AI 做重构Step 4: Have the AI Do the Refactoring

重构是 AI 最容易帮倒忙的地方，因为它"看到哪里不顺就改哪里"，而重构最忌讳的就是顺手改别的。下面这个对比，是我用一次 800 行的真实教训换来的。 Refactoring is where AI most often helps backwards, because it "fixes whatever looks wrong" — and refactoring is precisely the moment you must not change anything else. The comparison below came from one real 800-line mistake I made.

会出事的请求The request that goes wrong

"重构一下 invoice 模块，让代码更清晰。"

结果是：它重写了 800 行代码，顺手"优化"了 12 处业务逻辑，其中 3 处改变了行为。全部测试通过——因为那些测试覆盖的是它自己刚刚改掉的语义。这一句话让我花了 4 个小时回滚。 "Refactor the invoice module, make it cleaner."

The result: it rewrote 800 lines and "improved" 12 pieces of business logic, 3 of which changed behavior. Every test passed — because those tests now covered the semantics it had just changed. That one sentence cost me four hours of rollback.

能安全执行的请求The request that is safe

给出拆分目标（拆成哪几个文件、按什么依据拆）、边界（只读这个目录）、验收标准（现有测试必须全绿、行为完全不变）、过程约束（每拆一个就跑一次测试，失败就停）、以及禁止项（不要改命名、格式、任何没被要求的逻辑）。

同样的 800 行，这次只用了 35 分钟，diff 干净，业务行为零变化。 Give it a split target (which files, split on what basis), a boundary (read only this directory), an acceptance criterion (existing tests stay green, behavior identical), a process constraint (run tests after each split, stop on failure), and a prohibition list (don't rename, don't reformat, don't touch logic you weren't asked about).

Same 800 lines: 35 minutes, a clean diff, zero behavior change. 

重构任务：把 src/features/invoice/list.tsx 拆成 3 个文件。

约束：

第 4 条是安全阀Constraint 4 Is the Safety Net

"每一步跑一次测试，失败就停"这九个字，能把一次可能持续半小时的失控变成一次 30 秒的中断。我后来把它写进了 CLAUDE.md，对所有任务生效。Those words — "run the tests after each step and stop on failure" — turn a possible half-hour runaway into a 30-second interruption. I later moved them into CLAUDE.md so they apply to every task, not just refactors.

把这 6 行加总，我的结论是：AI 不会欠债，它只是不还债。欠下的债，来源是你给的模糊指令。没有测试，是因为你说"先实现功能"；有重复实现，是因为你没限定它读哪些文件；有死代码，是因为你从不要求它删东西。Adding up those six rows, my conclusion is: the AI doesn't incur debt — it just doesn't pay it off. The debt comes from vague instructions. No tests because you said "build the feature first." Duplicates because you didn't scope its reads. Dead code because you never asked it to delete anything.

再换个说法：技术债的本质是"未来的你"要为今天省下的时间付利息。我粗略估算过，每 1000 行 AI 生成的代码，后续维护成本大约是 4 到 6 小时。按 13.5 万行算，这笔"利息"在 540 到 810 小时之间——相当于两到三个月的工作时间，全部是利息。 Put differently: technical debt means your future self pays interest on the time you saved today. My rough estimate is 4–6 hours of maintenance per 1,000 lines of AI-generated code. At 135,000 lines, that interest runs 540–810 hours — two to three months of work, all of it interest.

一个很反直觉的事实A Counterintuitive Fact

我做过一个对照实验：同样 3 个功能，一个用 AI 写完再补测试，另一个让 AI 先写测试再写实现。前者总耗时 4.2 小时，后者 4.6 小时——后者多花 24 分钟。但三个月后我要给第一个加一个新字段时，第二个的改动用了 18 分钟，第一个用了 3 小时。那 24 分钟不是开销，是订阅。I ran a controlled comparison: the same three features, one written by AI then backfilled with tests, the other with tests written first. The first took 4.2 hours total, the second 4.6 — 24 minutes more. Three months later, adding one new field took 18 minutes in the second project and 3 hours in the first. Those 24 minutes weren't a cost; they were a subscription.

从"能跑"到"能维护"：一份检查清单From "It Runs" to "It Can Be Maintained": A Checklist

这份清单是我把 13.5 万行的项目从"能跑"整理到"能维护"时逐条写下来的。每一条都可以在十分钟内验证完。建议每两周过一遍，不要等到项目失控了才回头看。 This checklist came from actually taking a 135,000-line project from "it runs" to "it can be maintained." Every item takes under ten minutes to verify. Run through it every two weeks rather than waiting until the project is out of control.

  • 每个目录都有唯一职责，且职责写进了规则文件Every directory has one responsibility, and it's written in the rules file
  • 规则文件不超过 60 行，超了就删旧的The rules file is under 60 lines; if it's over, delete old lines
  • 每个公开函数至少有一个测试，核心业务路径覆盖率不低于 70%Every public function has at least one test; core paths at 70%+ coverage
  • 没有超过 100 行的函数No function exceeds 100 lines
  • 没有 3 处以上重复的逻辑No logic duplicated in more than three places
  • 密钥和 URL 全部走配置，没有硬编码All secrets and URLs come from config, none hardcoded
  • AI 每次提交的 diff 不超过 400 行，超过就要求它拆成多次Each AI commit touches under 400 lines; if more, split it
  • 每周有一次 45 分钟"AI 不参与"的代码阅读One 45-minute code-reading session per week with no AI involved
  • 三个月后的你（或一个新人）只读规则文件就能上手You in three months — or a new hire — can start from the rules file alone
  • 依赖清单里没有超过 6 个月没更新的库No dependency in the manifest hasn't been updated in 6+ months
  • CI 能跑通，而且 AI 写完代码必须自己先跑一次 CICI passes, and the AI always runs CI before handing work back
  • 每三个月做一次"如果现在重写还来得及吗"的评估Every three months: ask "if we rewrote today, would it still be worth it?"

最后一条看起来像鸡汤，其实是唯一一条需要你做判断而不是让 AI 做的。它不是在问"代码好不好"，而是在问"这个项目还在值得做吗"。如果答案是"不确定"，那么正确的决定可能是停下来重构，而不是继续用 AI 加速。 The last item sounds like motivational fluff, but it's the only one that requires your judgment rather than the AI's. It isn't asking "is the code good" — it's asking "is this project still worth doing?" If the answer is "unsure," the right move may be to stop and restructure rather than keep accelerating with AI.

我踩过的三个坑Three Mistakes I Actually Made

这一节可能比前面的方法论更有用，因为方法论到处都有，教训不容易。 This section may be more useful than the methods above, because methods are everywhere and lessons are not.

坑一：让它一次写完整个功能Mistake 1: Asking It to Build a Whole Feature at Once

"帮我实现完整的发票作废流程，包括前端按钮、后端接口、状态机和通知。" 它花了 40 分钟写了 6 个文件、900 行代码，看起来非常完整。问题是我没有先说清楚"作废"的三种情况（全部作废、部分作废、超时自动作废），它自己假设了只有一种，然后按自己的假设把状态机写死了。两小时后我让它加一个"部分作废"，它说我原来的设计不支持——那正是它自己设计的。返工花了我 3 小时。 "Implement the complete invoice voiding flow — frontend button, backend endpoint, state machine, and notification." It spent 40 minutes producing 6 files and 900 lines that looked complete. The problem was that I never said what "voiding" means: it has three cases (full void, partial void, auto-void on timeout). The AI assumed one, then hardcoded the state machine around its own assumption. Two hours later I asked for partial voiding, and it said the design I had "designed" didn't support it — the design it had written. The rework cost me three hours.

坑二：没有规则文件，每个新会话都要重新解释Mistake 2: No Rules File, So Every Session Started From Zero

项目前两个月我就是裸奔的。每次开新会话，都要重新交代"用 TypeScript、不要用 any、金额用分存、注释用中文、测试用 Vitest"。这个交代每次大约 2 分钟，一个项目 40 多次，累计超过 80 分钟。更贵的不是时间，是我漏掉某条规则的那些时候——比如有一次我没提"金额用分"，它就把金额写成了浮点数，我在对账的时候才发现少了 0.03 元。 For the first two months I ran with no rules file. Every new session meant re-explaining: "use TypeScript, no any, store money in cents, comment in Chinese, test with Vitest." That briefing took about two minutes; over forty sessions it added up to more than 80 minutes. The expensive part wasn't the time — it was the sessions where I forgot a rule. Once I didn't mention "store money in cents," it wrote floats, and I found the missing $0.03 during reconciliation.

坑三：把"测试通过"当成"没有 bug"Mistake 3: Treating "Tests Pass" as "No Bugs"

覆盖率数字一度涨到 43%，我以为安全了。后来线上出了 3 个 bug，全部集中在最关键的开票、作废、退款三条路径上——而这三条路径恰好是我从来没让 AI 写测试的地方，因为"主流程都跑通了，测试补一补就行"。覆盖率高的地方都是简单的地方，测试没覆盖的地方才是会赔钱的地方。 Coverage once climbed to 43% and I felt safe. Then three bugs shipped to production, all on the three most critical paths: issuing, voiding, and refunding — and those were exactly the paths I'd never had the AI write tests for, because "the main flow works, I'll backfill tests later." Coverage was high on the easy parts; the untested parts were the parts that cost money.

三个坑的共同点What the Three Mistakes Have in Common

回看这三个坑，触发条件是同一个：我把 AI 当成了"提效工具"，而没有当成"一个新的实习生"。新来的实习生不知道你的规矩，你不告诉他；你不说清楚需求，他就按自己的理解做；你不检查他的代码，他留的错就进了主干。对 AI 也要做同样的事——只不过它学得更快，也忘得更快。Looking back, all three share one trigger: I treated the AI as a productivity tool rather than a new intern. A new intern doesn't know your conventions unless you tell them; hand them an unclear spec and they'll do what they think is right; don't check their work and their mistakes land in main. The same applies to AI — it just learns faster, and forgets faster too.

如果你刚开始：一个 4 周的落地节奏If You're Starting: A Four-Week Plan

不用一次做完所有事。下面这四周，每周只做一件事，每件大概半天到一天。 You don't have to do everything at once. Four weeks, one thing each, roughly half a day to a full day per week.

第 1 周：整理目录，写规则文件Week 1: Tidy the Directories, Write the Rules File

把现有项目按职责重新分目录，一个目录不超过 4000 行。然后写 CLAUDE.md，只写"不知道就会做错"的六类信息，控制在 60 行内。这一周结束时，你的 AI 会明显变准——如果没变准，说明目录还没切干净。Re-slice the project by responsibility, no directory over 4,000 lines. Then write CLAUDE.md with only the six categories of information the AI would get wrong otherwise, under 60 lines. By week's end your AI should be visibly sharper — if it isn't, the directories aren't clean yet.

第 2 周：补齐三条核心路径的测试Week 2: Cover the Three Critical Paths

不要追求覆盖率数字，只挑你最怕出问题的三条业务路径，用上面那个"先写测试"的提示词范式把它们写出来。覆盖率可能只从 20% 涨到 30%，但你的三条主流程从此有了保护。Don't chase a coverage number. Pick the three business paths you're most afraid of breaking and write them with the test-first prompt template. Coverage might only go from 20% to 30%, but your three main flows now have a safety net.

第 3 周：清理重复实现和死代码Week 3: Clear Out Duplicates and Dead Code

用体检报告里那张表当清单，一次处理一类。每一次重构都用那个带 6 条约束的提示词。这一周通常能消掉一半的重复实现，工作区的噪音会明显下降。Use the audit table as your worklist, one category at a time. Every refactor uses that 6-constraint prompt. This week usually clears half the duplicates, and the noise in your workspace drops noticeably.

第 4 周：把清单变成每周习惯Week 4: Turn the Checklist Into a Weekly Habit

在日历上固定每周 45 分钟，关掉所有 AI 工具，只读 diff、跑测试、过一遍清单。这一步没有任何技术含量，但它才是让前面三周不白做的关键。Put a recurring 45-minute block on your calendar: every AI tool closed, just read diffs, run tests, walk the checklist. There's nothing technical about it, and it's what keeps the previous three weeks from being wasted.

写在最后Closing Thoughts

vibe coding 的入门文章会告诉你"先跑起来再说"，这句话在 5000 行以内是对的，在 10 万行以后是灾难。因为代码量一大，"跑起来"的成本就会由别人来付——由那个三个月后回来、发现 AI 留下的 47 处重复实现和 236 个死代码的你来付。 Vibe-coding primers tell you to "get it running first." That's right up to about 5,000 lines, and a disaster past 100,000. Because as the codebase grows, the cost of "it runs" gets paid by someone else — by you, three months later, staring at 47 duplicate implementations and 236 dead exports.

但我想说的是另一面：10 万行不是一道墙，它是一道可以爬过去的坡。让 AI 写 10 万行难，但让 AI 在一个结构清晰、规则明确、测试扎实的 10 万行项目里继续工作，并不难。区别只在于你提前做了哪几件事：分好目录、写好规则、让它先写测试、每周自己看一眼。 But here's the other side: 100,000 lines isn't a wall, it's a slope you can climb. Getting AI to write 100,000 lines is hard; getting AI to keep working inside a well-structured, clearly-ruled, well-tested 100,000-line project is not. The difference is entirely in what you set up beforehand: clean directories, written rules, tests first, and a weekly look with your own eyes.

AI 写得快，是你的项目跑得快的唯一原因，也是你项目失控的唯一原因。区别在方向，不在速度。 AI writing fast is the only reason your project moves quickly — and the only reason it can go out of control. The difference is direction, not speed.
